To My Star 💫

To my star, Claire.

In a cosmos of billions, I found you—a gravitational anomaly in the quiet vastness of space. It’s only been three months—a flicker in universal time—yet you've become the constellation I search for every night. I don’t know how it happened. One moment I was orbiting normally, the next, I was falling—fast, hard, and completely. Like a rogue satellite pulled off course, I crash into thoughts of you more often than I mean to. Your presence feels otherworldly—like you’re made of stardust and gravity, pulling me in without warning. I’ve tried to say this a thousand times, but the words always scatter like meteor dust. So let me say it now, under pixel stars and quiet wishes: You are the signal I’ve been waiting for. In the void I once floated alone, you've become my beacon. If you feel even a spark from this—maybe we can drift together. Through galaxies of laughter and quiet starlit silences. If not, let this be a message in a bottle, cast into the cosmic sea—because I had to tell you: you make the universe less lonely. Yours Truly,
Kael.
